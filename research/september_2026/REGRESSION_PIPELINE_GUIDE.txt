Bundled fixtures/pipeline/ contains source.csv, acquisition.json, SPEC.json, clarification.json and provenance hashes. These hand-defined prices are not market data. Alternatively supply a compatible explicitly labeled synthetic fixture.

Five actual-tool cases: valid end-to-end run; source/capture race; proof/report replay swap; saved proof mutation; saved capture mutation. Six labeled contract cases: consistent success, REFUTED+zero exit, SURVIVES+nonzero exit, explicit proof failure, wrong capture digest and missing report artifacts. The valid run must succeed first; otherwise the harness stops and refuses to count blanket refusals as correct behavior.

Each case preserves logs, captures and CHECK.json; RESULT.json gives11 test outcomes and held code hashes. Exit0 means these bounded checks passed, not strategy/teacher/live-money clearance. Prior failed reviews remain separately preserved.
