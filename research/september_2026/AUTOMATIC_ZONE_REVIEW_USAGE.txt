Automatic local zone review v1

AutonomousZoneReview(source_sha256, symbol, feed, step=300) consumes one
observed native bar at a time via feed_bar(). The required bar schema is the
same as EstablishedZoneReview. It takes no selected zone, origin timestamp,
reference timestamp, drawing, confirmation label, or future outcome.

It derives local swing references, completed-close breaks and two fixed origin
interpretations from the observed prefix. Identical candidates are deduplicated.
Distinct singleton/cluster alternatives remain unresolved. Subsequent overlapping
candles are observations, not independent return episodes or trades. Gaps block
existing review zones and reset discovery; ambiguous reversal bars reset local
swing discovery without erasing already established review observations.

The frozen engine and entry/exit adapters are unchanged. This separate research
component does not emit ENTRY_REVIEW, prove parent authority, authorize execution,
or decide which alternative is the teacher-correct formation. R5 and close-cross
remain explicitly assumed local diagnostic conventions. See the source-bound
AUTOMATIC_ZONE_REVIEW_20261024.json before interpreting a match.
